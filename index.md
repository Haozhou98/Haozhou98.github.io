I try to tell when machines are about to break, in a way engineers can trust.

I'm a PhD candidate in industrial engineering at Northeastern, working on condition monitoring for critical energy equipment. Mostly that means building failure predictors that can explain themselves and are cheap enough to actually run.

I also teach data mining and manufacturing labs.
